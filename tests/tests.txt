# Tests

## Test 1 - Home converts directly from family choice [sanity]
Viewport: mobile
Covers: conversion-first homepage, family cover chooser, quote entry, customer-facing copy
Description: Verifies Home presents the family-protection choice prominently without explaining the website's design intent.

Steps:
1. Load Home and scroll to "Who needs cover?"
2. Confirm "Just me", "Me + partner", "My family" and "Extended family" are visible
3. Select "My family"

Expected: The personalised quote journey opens with "My family" carried forward, and no text on Home says "the homepage gets you" or explains where website content is placed.

## Test 2 - Premium mobile navigation reaches every primary destination
Viewport: mobile
Covers: persistent primary navigation, Home, Funeral Cover, Funeral Services, Churches, About, Contact, route reliability
Description: Verifies primary destinations are visible without opening a hamburger menu and each route opens its intended page.

1. Use the visible top navigation strip to open Funeral Cover, Services and Churches
2. Continue to About and Contact
3. Use Home to return to the landing page

Expected: Every destination renders its matching page heading and Home returns to the conversion landing page without a broken or stale route.

## Test 3 - Legal information is complete inside the replacement
Viewport: desktop
Covers: internal Terms & Conditions, internal Privacy Policy, no old-site redirects, legal completeness guardrail
Description: Verifies customers can read substantive legal information without being sent to the previous Java Legacy website.

Steps:
1. Open Terms & Conditions from the footer and inspect the full page
2. Open Privacy Policy from the footer and inspect the full page
3. Inspect both pages for any action that opens javalegacyfunerals.co.za/terms or javalegacyfunerals.co.za/privacy

Expected: Terms includes product, quotation, waiting-period, service, privacy, complaints and governing-law sections; Privacy includes collection, purpose, sharing, retention, security and rights sections; neither page contains an old-site redirect action.

# Tests

## Test 1 - Mobile Home converts cleanly into the family quote [sanity]
Viewport: mobile
Covers: conversion-first Home, compact mobile hero, family chooser, quote modal, mobile sticky actions
Description: Verifies the primary mobile journey reaches the family chooser without UI obstruction and opens the accepted quote experience.

Steps:
1. Load Home and scroll to "Who needs cover?"
2. Select "My family"
3. Inspect the open quote dialog and then close it

Expected: The quote opens with "Cover for: My family", the dialog fits within the mobile viewport, and the bottom "Need help now / Get Cover" actions are hidden while the dialog is open and return after it closes.

## Test 2 - Hamburger navigation is unobstructed across restored destinations
Viewport: mobile
Covers: sticky utility/header, hamburger menu, Team, Gallery, Enquiries, Contact, overlay behavior
Description: Verifies the premium mobile menu remains usable after scrolling and is not covered by the persistent bottom actions.

Steps:
1. Scroll Home, open the hamburger menu and navigate to Our Team
2. Reopen the hamburger and navigate to Gallery and Enquiries
3. Reopen it and navigate to Contact

Expected: The menu begins directly beneath the sticky Java header, the bottom conversion bar does not cover the open menu, each destination renders correctly, and the menu closes after navigation.

## Test 3 - Quote and assistance dialogs preserve usable progress and guardrails
Viewport: desktop
Covers: quote dialog progress, modal keyboard-ready structure, disabled selection guardrail, funeral assistance progress
Description: Verifies the production modal layer clearly communicates progress and prevents moving forward before a required choice is made.

Steps:
1. Open Get a Quote with no family selection
2. Confirm Continue is disabled, then choose "Just me" and continue
3. Close the quote, open funeral assistance and inspect its step indicator

Expected: The quote shows "Step 1 of 5", Continue is unavailable until a family choice is selected, and funeral assistance visibly shows "Step 1 of 4" with immediate-help actions available for a death.

## Test 4 - Core commercial journeys remain independent
Viewport: desktop
Covers: Funeral Cover, funeral assistance, Church Partnership, WhatsApp handoffs, route separation
Description: Verifies UX hardening does not merge or break Java's three commercial customer journeys.

Steps:
1. From Funeral Cover start the personalised quote
2. From Funeral Services choose "A loved one has passed away"
3. From Churches open "Discuss a partnership"

Expected: Each action opens its own correct workflow; bereavement assistance exposes call/WhatsApp help immediately, while cover and church enquiries remain separate.

## Test 5 - Information, trust and legal routes remain complete
Viewport: desktop
Covers: About, Team, Gallery, Enquiries, Contact, Terms, Privacy, internal navigation regression
Description: Verifies the production-readiness pass preserves the full replacement-site information architecture.

Steps:
